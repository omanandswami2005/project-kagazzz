    How it works

    1. 1

      Scan or upload

      Point your camera. Kagaz crops, deskews, and saves to your vault.

    2. 2

      Speak to Mitra

      Ask in Hindi or English: “मेरा PAN दिखाओ”, “merge these”, “fill this form from my Aadhaar.”

    3. ✓

      Done.

      Merged. Compressed. Signed. Submitted. Your work is done.

    Features

    🗄️

    Your papers, encrypted on your phone.

    Aadhaar, PAN, marksheet, passport — sorted, searchable, never leaves your device.

    🤖

    An assistant that speaks your language.

    Voice or text, Hindi or English. Merges, signs, fills forms — all on-device. No cloud, no listening.

    🔗

    Share with a 5-digit code. V2

    Send your marksheet to your university. The link dies in 24 hours. You stay in control.

    🏛️

    Government services without the queue. V3

    Apply for PAN, update Aadhaar, book an Ayushman card — from your couch, with Mitra guiding you.

    Your papers stay on your phone. Always.

    We don’t have your Aadhaar. We can’t have it. Kagaz runs entirely on your device — the AI, the search, the encryption. There is no Kagaz server that holds your documents.

    When you share (V2), only you decide who, how long, and what they can do.
