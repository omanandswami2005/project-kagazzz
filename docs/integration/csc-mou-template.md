# CSC (Common Service Centre) Partnering & MOU Template — Kagaz (Omni Docs)

Last updated: 2026-06-08

Purpose

This file provides a template MOU and onboarding checklist to partner with CSC SPV or state-level CSC nodal agencies. Use it when you plan to surface CSCs in the app, enable CSC-assisted flows, or list CSC fees.

1. Onboarding checklist

- Reach out to CSC SPV / state nodal agency with product brief
- Provide privacy policy, security docs, and training plan
- Agree commercial terms (referral fees, listing fees, or free listing)
- Define support & escalation matrix
- Pilot in selected districts

2. Sample MOU clauses (high level)

Parties: Omni Docs Pvt Ltd ("Omni Docs") and [CSC SPV / State Agency] ("Partner").

- Purpose: Pilot and integrate CSC listings and CSC-assisted workflows into Kagaz.
- Responsibilities Omni Docs:
  - Provide accurate CSC listings and maintain periodic updates.
  - Implement CSC consent flows for any data shared.
  - Provide training materials & onboarding for CSC operators.
- Responsibilities Partner:
  - Provide authorized CSC data and support for verification.
  - Assist in operator outreach and pilot coordination.
- Data sharing & privacy:
  - Data shared with CSCs will be only with explicit user consent and for a limited purpose.
  - Both parties will comply with applicable laws (Aadhaar Act, DPDP, IT Act).
- Fees & revenue share (if any): [define]
- Audit & compliance: The partner may audit Omni Docs for compliance with security and privacy obligations.
- Term & termination: [define]
- Dispute resolution & governing law: India, courts in [City]

3. Operational matters

- Training program: remote + in-person modules
- Sandbox: test CSC-assisted flows before production
- Fee transparency: show CSC service fees at point of transaction

4. Consent flow sample (user-facing)

"Do you want to send your document to [CSC Name] for [service]? This will share [list fields] with the CSC. [Cancel] [Allow]"

---

Note: This is a starting MOU. Use legal counsel for binding agreements.
