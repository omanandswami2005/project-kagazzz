kagazzz — logo concepts, round 2

Six directions, each with its own colour, built the way the best-known app marks are: GitHub (white on graphite), Claude (one warm colour), Spotify and WhatsApp (one owned colour), Instagram (one gradient, one glyph). Every concept is checked on white, in dark mode, as a black silhouette, at 24 px, as a wordmark and on a home screen.

  • One silhouetteRecognisable from the outline alone; see each card's black "silhouette test".
  • One owned colourA dominant background, one symbol colour, at most one accent.
  • No text in the iconText is unreadable at icon size; the name goes in the wordmark.
  • Crisp edgesFlat shapes, no soft glows, so iOS and Android effects and tinted modes render cleanly.
  • Works at 24 pxNotification icons, settings lists and share sheets are tiny.
  • No official symbolsNo national emblem or flag on a private app.

Kashti

Saffron

“Kaagaz ki kashti”: the paper boat every Indian child has folded. Paper that carries what matters safely.

Warm and confident, like Claude’s terracotta. Saffron is bright, Indian and nothing like the blue or green of most document apps. One white silhouette; the fold line is the only detail.
