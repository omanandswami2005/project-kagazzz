Kagazzz Docs — logo, round 3

New in round 3: cards 1–2 are your pick, the paper K from concept 2 in the saffron of concept 1, with a small z (Kz), and the app name is now Kagazzz Docs. The earlier directions follow for comparison, each with its own colour, built the way the best-known app marks are: GitHub (white on graphite), Claude (one warm colour), Spotify and WhatsApp (one owned colour), Instagram (one gradient, one glyph). Every concept is checked on white, in dark mode, as a black silhouette, at 24 px, as a wordmark and on a home screen.

  • One silhouetteRecognisable from the outline alone; see each card's black "silhouette test".
  • One owned colourA dominant background, one symbol colour, at most one accent.
  • No text in the iconText is unreadable at icon size; the name goes in the wordmark.
  • Crisp edgesFlat shapes, no soft glows, so iOS and Android effects and tinted modes render cleanly.
  • Works at 24 pxNotification icons, settings lists and share sheets are tiny.
  • No official symbolsNo national emblem or flag on a private app.
