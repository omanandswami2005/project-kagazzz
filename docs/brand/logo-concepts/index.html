Kagazzz — logo concepts

Six directions, each drawn as code (scripts/brand/build-logo-concepts.mjs) in the brand gradient (indigo → violet → fuchsia) with the green "safe" accent. Each one is shown as the app icon, on light and dark, at small sizes, as a wordmark lockup, and on a home screen. Pick one or two to refine.

  • One ideaGreat product marks say one thing (Dropbox: an open box; Google Drive: the drive triangle). Each concept here carries one story.
  • Works at 24 pxApp icons live as small tiles, so every concept has a simplified small form, shown at 40 and 24 px.
  • Ownable meaningGeneric "document + check" icons all look alike. Kagazzz has unique hooks: कागज़, the paper boat, the zzz.
  • Fits the brandUses the existing gradient tokens and the green accent, so the app UI does not change.
  • Trust for ID documentsThe app holds Aadhaar and PAN, so the mark must feel safe and calm, not playful only.
  • No official symbolsNo Ashoka emblem or national flag. Using them on a private app is restricted, and it would mislead users.

Kashti — the paper boat

“Kaagaz ki kashti”

64 · 40 · 24 px
On a home screen next to other apps

Every Indian child has folded a paper boat. A boat made of paper that safely carries what matters across the water, like your documents carried safely on your phone.

Strongest cultural story and instantly recognisable. The folds say "paper"; the green wave is the accent. Reduces to two shapes at 24 px.

Paper + India in one glance

Folded K

A monogram folded from paper strips

64 · 40 · 24 px
On a home screen next to other apps

The K of Kagazzz built from three paper strips. One strip folds over another, and the shading shows the crease. Simple, geometric and ownable.

Works best as a pure monogram for the app icon, the splash and the notification icon. Least literal, so it needs the wordmark nearby at first.

Mohar — the verified seal

Official, stamped, safe

64 · 40 · 24 px
On a home screen next to other apps

A document carrying a round मोहर (stamp) with a tick. Indian paperwork lives and dies by the seal; here the seal means verified and protected.

Most "government documents" in feel and builds trust quickly. Watch that it does not look like an official government emblem; keep the seal generic, with no Ashoka or state symbols.

Paper shield

Privacy-first, literally

64 · 40 · 24 px
On a home screen next to other apps

A shield whose corner folds like a sheet of paper, with a keyhole in the middle. Your documents, locked, on your phone, with nothing leaving it.

Says "privacy and security" fastest, the core promise of the app. Shields are common in security apps, so the paper fold is what makes it ours.

Zzz stack

Sleep easy: your papers are sorted

64 · 40 · 24 px
On a home screen next to other apps

Three stacked sheets, like the zzz in Kagazzz, with a bold Z on top. It makes the name memorable and promises calm: your documents are in order, so you can relax.

The most playful and the most memorable for the "Kagazzz" spelling. Less serious than Mohar or Shield, which suits a friendly consumer app.
